Onboarding with a languages step

A new “Your languages” step checks whether OraType has a suggested setup for the languages you speak. If it does, the Speech recognition and AI polish steps start with those models chosen; the rest of onboarding is unchanged. Click through with Next, or jump to a step below.

What changes
  • New step, Your languages, after Voice input. It only looks up whether a suggested setup exists for the combination. Today there is one: any combination with Cantonese gets Qwen3-ASR 1.7B and Qwen 3.5 4B. Other combinations keep the standard models.
  • Speech recognition and AI polish keep their Built-in card and per-step download. The list now has three models instead of two, and the suggested one is chosen and tagged “Suggested for your languages”.
  • No quality labels per language. Settings lists the models with size only; the suggestion tag appears only on the model from a suggested setup.
  • Home shows a “Suggested for your languages” group, styled like Finish setup, while a model from the matching setup is not in use. “Set up” opens Settings (nothing downloads from Home); “Don’t show again” hides it on Home for good.
  • Welcome, Voice input, Web search and the shortcut tour are unchanged.

What the mock shows
  • The model catalogue grows from two sizes per step to models picked by language. Qwen3-ASR 1.7B and Qwen 3.5 4B are the pair that runs on the PC today.
  • Fit labels per language come from the language evals. Only “Written Chinese” for Whisper on Cantonese is measured so far (about 44 % character error on the eval clips); the others are proposals.
  • Qwen3-ASR runs in a second copy of the built-in AI server, so it needs no new runtime. Memory limits pick the faster AI model on 8 GB Macs.
